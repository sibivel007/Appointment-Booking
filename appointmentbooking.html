A small clinic built around specialists

ZFH Healthcare runs as three focused departments rather than one general queue. You see the doctor who treats your condition every day, and your records stay with that team between visits.

Same-week appointments

Every doctor keeps slots open through the week, so most patients are seen within three days of booking.

One record, every visit

Test results, prescriptions and notes stay linked to your file, whichever department you visit next.

Follow-up included

A review call after your consultation is part of the visit, not a separate appointment you have to chase.
